# Drink Quick Cal — User Manual

*For everyone who sells, serves or counts: staff, cashiers, bartenders and managers. Version 2.0 — 27 September 2026.*

Drink Quick Cal is a bar and drinks management app for phones and tablets. It records what you sell, keeps the stock right, follows customers who owe money, and closes the day's cash-up. It works **offline**: if the network drops, keep selling, and the app synchronises when the connection comes back.

The app is available in **English and French** — change it in *Settings → Language*. All amounts are shown in **XAF** unless your company set another currency.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Install the app** from the link or store page your manager gives you.
2. **Open it and sign in** with the username and password your manager created for you. You cannot create your own staff account.
3. **Choose your plan screen** — the first time you sign in you may see the plans page. Tap **Continue on Free** to carry on without paying, or pay for a plan (see §11).
4. **Set a device PIN** when the app asks. On a shared till the PIN plus the lock screen stop the next person using your session.
5. **Let the app ask for permissions only when needed**: camera for scanning a drink barcode, Bluetooth for printing, notifications for low-stock and expiry alerts. You can refuse any of them.

> **Tip.** Sign out at the end of your shift (see §13) so your floats and sales are not mixed with the next person's.

## 3. The Dashboard

The dashboard gives you the day at a glance: total sales, number of orders, cash on hand, what is owed by customers, drinks running low, and items expiring soon. Tap any card to jump to the matching screen. Pull down (or tap refresh) to update; if you are offline the app shows the last known figures and marks them accordingly.

## 4. Recording a sale (Calculator)

1. Open **Calculator**.
2. **Tap the drinks** you sold. Search by name, or tap **Scan** to read a barcode.
3. **Adjust quantities** with the + / − buttons. Remove a line by tapping the bin icon.
4. **Choose the customer** if the sale is on credit: pick an existing customer or add a new one (name and phone are enough; address is optional). For a walk-in cash sale you can leave it empty.
5. **Apply a discount** if your role allows it. Above the limit your manager set, the app asks a manager to approve with their password.
6. **Choose how it is paid**: cash, mobile money, card, or **on credit** (onto the customer's tab). If the customer pays only part, enter the amount received — the rest stays as a balance on the customer's record.
7. **Confirm the sale.** The app generates an invoice number, reduces stock, and offers the receipt.

**If the network is down**, the sale is saved on the device with a pending mark and uploaded automatically later. Do not re-enter a sale because you do not see it online yet — check the sync indicator in the app bar first.

## 5. Taking the money

- **Cash** — enter what you received; the app shows the change to give.
- **Mobile money** — select the channel and enter or confirm the payer's number, then confirm once the payment is actually received.
- **Card** — same idea, when a card channel is configured for your company.
- **Credit / tab** — the amount is added to the customer's balance in **Customers**. Nothing is written off: the balance stays until it is settled.
- **Mixed payment** — enter the part paid now; the remainder becomes the customer's balance.

> **Rule of honesty.** Every sale, discount, void and credit note is recorded with your name and the time. Corrections are made by *voiding with a reason* (managers only), never by editing an old invoice.
## 6. Receipts, printing and WhatsApp

After a sale the app offers the receipt:

- **Print** — on a paired Bluetooth thermal printer. First time: *Settings → Printing*, switch the printer on and pair it, then choose it as the default. Keep the printer charged and within a few metres.
- **Send on WhatsApp** — the app writes the message (items, total, balance) and opens WhatsApp; you choose the contact. Nothing is sent automatically.
- **PDF / share** — create a PDF of the receipt or the invoice and share it by any app on the phone.
- **No printer?** the receipt is still saved with the order, so a manager can print it later.

## 7. Invoice History — checking and re-sending past sales

Open **Invoice History** to see past sales, newest first. Search by invoice number, customer or date, and filter by day, shift or payment type. For any sale you can:

- **View** the full detail: items, quantities, prices, discount, payment, who served it.
- **Reprint** the receipt on the thermal printer.
- **Re-send** it on WhatsApp or as a PDF.
- **Void it** — managers only, and a reason is mandatory (see the Admin manual, §2). A voided sale is greyed out with the reason, the manager's name and the time, and the stock is put back. Voided sales are excluded from the day's takings.

> Nothing in the history can be edited. If a colleague made a mistake, ask a manager to void the sale and record the correct one.

## 8. Customers and credit (tabs)

**Customers** holds everyone who buys on credit:

1. **Add a customer** — name and phone are required; address, note and a credit limit are optional. The app gives each customer a short number.
2. **Open a tab** — sell on credit from the Calculator, or add a manual entry from the customer's page (an advance, a repayment, an adjustment).
3. **Settle a debt** — open the customer, tap **Pay**, choose how much is paid (cash, mobile money, card), and confirm. The balance updates immediately and stays in the ledger.
4. **Follow up** — the customer page shows the ledger (every sale, payment and adjustment with dates), the outstanding balance and the last activity. Tap **WhatsApp** to send a polite reminder with the amount.
5. **Credit limit** — if a customer reaches the limit you set, the app warns you before the sale is confirmed.

**Expected balance**, not guesswork: the ledger is the record of truth. If a customer disputes an amount, open the ledger together and read line by line.

## 9. Drinks Management

- **Add a drink**: name, category, buying (cost) price and selling price, unit, and an optional photo. Scanning a barcode fills the code for you.
- **Prices**: the app can hold a retail and a wholesale/second price where your company uses two price levels. Cost price is hidden from staff who should not see margins.
- **Edit / archive**: change a price at any time. Archiving hides a drink from the Calculator without deleting its history.
- **Image**: take a photo with the camera or choose one from the gallery — the picture helps staff tap the right bottle.

## 10. Inventory and stock

- **Stock levels** — every drink shows quantity on hand, the value at cost, and the reorder level. Low items are flagged in red and listed on the dashboard.
- **Receiving stock** — record a delivery (supplier, quantity, unit cost) and the level rises with a movement line for the audit trail.
- **Adjustments** — spillage, breakage, a gift to a customer, or a counting correction: record it with a reason. Levels never change silently.
- **Expiry** — set the expiry date when you receive stock. The app alerts you before items go out of date and lists them on the dashboard.
- **Movements** — the movement list shows every in and out, who did it and when. Use it when a number looks wrong.

## 11. Shift and cash-up

1. **Open a shift** and enter the **float** (cash in the drawer at the start). The app remembers it and warns you if a shift is already open.
2. **Sell** as normal during the shift. Cash, mobile money, card and credit are tracked separately.
3. **Close the shift**: count the cash in the drawer and enter it in **Counted cash**. The app compares:
   - **Expected cash** = float + cash sales − payouts,
   - **Difference** = counted − expected.
4. Add a **note** explaining any difference, then close. The **Z report** is produced for that shift: sales by payment type, discounts, credit given, voids excluded, and the cash difference.
5. **Shift history** keeps every closed shift for reference and reprinting.

> A missing amount is a signal, not a secret. Write the reason in the note and tell your manager the same day; unexplained gaps are what stock and cash controls are designed to catch.

## 12. Variance — counting the stock properly

**Variance** compares what the system believes you should have with what you physically counted:

1. Start a count (for the whole bar or a category).
2. Enter the counted quantity for each drink, or confirm the expected quantity where they match.
3. The app shows the difference and its value at cost for every line, then the total variance.
4. Add a reason where there is a meaningful gap (spillage, unrecorded sale, mis-pour, transfer).
5. Save the count: adjustments are applied with your name, so the next count starts from a clean base.

Count regularly (weekly is a good habit) and always before a stock delivery or after a busy weekend.
## 13. Reports: Analytics, Forecast and exports

**Analytics** shows sales over a period (day, week, month, custom), best sellers, sales by category, sales by payment type, and the split by staff or shift where your company uses them. **Forecast** uses your own history to suggest what to buy next and flags days or drinks that look unusual.

Both screens export: **PDF** for printing or sending, **Excel** for further work, **CSV** for importing into another tool. Choose the period first, then export — the export always matches what is on screen.

## 14. Notifications and expiry alerts

Turn on notifications to receive:

- **low-stock** warnings when a drink falls to its reorder level,
- **expiry** reminders for stock you received,
- **shift** reminders (a shift left open too long),
- **sync** problems, so a device that cannot reach the server does not sit quietly out of date.

Tap a notification to open the matching screen. You can silence any category in *Settings → Notifications* or in Android settings.

## 15. Working offline, and synchronising

The app is built to keep working without the network:

- The sync indicator in the app bar shows **Offline**, **Syncing**, **Synced** or **Failed**.
- Sales, stock movements and payments you make offline wait on the device and upload automatically.
- Keep going on the same phone and the same account, and do not log out while records are pending.
- **If it says "Synced", everything you entered has reached the server.** If it stays red for a long time, check the phone's data connection and tell your manager; never clear the app data.
- Sales made on two devices at once are merged in the order they were confirmed; a ticket that looks wrong on one device can be checked in **Invoice History**.

## 16. AI Assistant

The AI Assistant answers plain questions about your own company's data — for example "what sold best last week?", "which drinks should I reorder?", "how much is owed by customers?", "how did this month compare to last month?". It only sees your company's figures, and its answers are suggestions: the reports and ledgers remain the record of truth.
## 17. Subscription and paying

Open **Subscription** to see your current plan and when it expires.

1. Tap **Choose plan** and pick a plan — the features included are listed on each card.
2. **Pay with MoMo** or **Pay with Orange**: enter the paying number and confirm the prompt on the phone. **Card** appears when your company has that channel.
3. After paying, the app shows *payment pending verification* until the operator confirms it, and then the plan switches on automatically. Tap refresh to check right away.
4. If a plan expires, paid features switch off and the app continues on the free plan. **Your records are kept** — nothing is deleted because a plan lapsed.
5. A **test-mode** banner means the payment channel is not live yet: tell your manager instead of paying repeatedly.

## 18. Profile and settings

- **Language** — English or French, applied immediately.
- **Theme** — light or dark; helpful under bar lighting.
- **Currency and number format** — followed everywhere money appears.
- **Device lock / PIN** — the app locks itself when idle; on a shared till this is your protection.
- **Company logo** — the owner uploads the logo that appears on receipts and invoices (see the Admin manual).
- **Storage settings** — how much is kept on the device, and clearing the local cache. Clearing the cache does not delete sales already synchronised, but ask your manager first.
- **Notifications** — see §14.

## 19. Problems and answers

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "I cannot sign in" | Check Caps Lock and the username from your manager; ask a manager to reset your password. Never share your password. |
| "The sale is not in history" | Check the sync indicator — it may still be pending offline. Do not sell it again. |
| "The printer prints nothing" | Printer on and charged? Paired in *Settings → Printing*? Close enough? Re-pair once, then retry. |
| "A stock number looks wrong" | Open **Inventory → Movements**, then do a **Variance** count with a reason. |
| "A customer disagrees about their tab" | Open the customer ledger and read the entries together; a manager can add an adjustment with a note. |
| "The app is slow" | Check the connection, let sync finish, and clear the local cache only with a manager's agreement. |
| "I paid but the plan is still free" | Confirmation can take a few minutes; refresh. If it stays pending, send the transaction reference to support. |
