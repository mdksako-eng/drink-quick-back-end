Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Drink Quick Cal ("we", "us", "the app") collects, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information

When you register, we collect your username, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), and the answers to your security questions.

b) Business information (for business owners/managers)

If you register a business, we collect your company name, company code, contact email, phone, and address. If you connect mobile-money payments, we store the payment configuration needed to process your transactions (we do not store your bank/card details).

c) Device & session information

To keep your account secure, we collect device identifiers, device names, and session records, and may log IP-level data. This powers features such as staff login approvals and detecting when a session is used on another device.

d) Business data you create

Drink menus, orders, inventory, stock transactions, payments, reports, and settings that you create or enter within the app are stored to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and business, and to verify your email.
  • To authenticate you securely and protect your account (including owner/manager verification of new members, and staff login approvals).
  • To process orders and payments, and to show you order, stock, and payment history.
  • To provide in-app notifications about your orders, stock, and payments.
  • To improve, secure, and maintain the app.

3. How We Store and Protect Data

  • Passwords are hashed (bcrypt) — we never store or see your plaintext password.
  • Sensitive application data is stored in a database protected by Row-Level Security (RLS), and is only accessed through our authenticated backend.
  • On mobile devices, local secure storage (such as PINs) is encrypted using the device's hardware security features.
  • Business payment credentials are kept server-side and never exposed to clients.
  • Verification codes and join approvals are time-limited and single-use.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in these limited cases:

  • Payment providers (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money): to process a payment you initiate, the relevant transaction details are submitted to the provider you choose.
  • Hosting providers that store data on our behalf (e.g., database and application hosting), under appropriate safeguards.
  • Legal requirements: to comply with a valid legal request or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Your Choices & Rights

  • You can update your account and business information at any time in the app.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • Company owners can manage the members of their business (approve or reject join requests, remove members).
  • You can delete your app-stored data (local storage) via the app's storage settings.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and business data for as long as your account is active. When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymise your personal data, except where we are required to keep records for legal or security purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for business and adult use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
