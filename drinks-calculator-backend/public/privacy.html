Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · Effective 27 September 2026 · Applies to the Drink Quick Cal app and the service behind it (the “Service”).

Drink Quick Cal is used by bars, restaurants and drinks retailers (each a “Shop”) to record sales, manage stock, follow customer credit and close the day’s cash-up. A till handles money and customers’ names, so this policy states plainly what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you get it out or deleted.

Plain-language summary. Your Shop owns its business data — drinks, orders, stock, customers, staff. We host and process it for you and never sell it. Staff accounts are created by the Shop’s manager, not by us. There is no advertising tracking, and we do not read your books for our own purposes.

1. Who we are

Drink Quick Cal (the “Service”) is operated by the Drink Quick Cal publisher, based in Douala, Cameroon (“we”, “us”). We decide how the Service itself is built, secured and operated; full legal identification of the publisher is available at the address in section 13.

2. Two roles: our data and your Shop’s data

DataWho decidesWhat that means
Account, staff, drinks, orders, stock, expensesYour ShopThe Shop controls it; we host and process it only to run the Service for that Shop.
Your Shop’s own customers — names, phones, addresses, tabs, purchase historyYour ShopThe Shop is responsible for telling its customers what it records and why. We act only on the Shop’s instructions and never use that data for ourselves.

Every record is scoped to one Shop, and access is enforced on the server from the signed-in session — never from anything the app sends.

3. What we collect

  • Account: name or username, email, optional phone, role, company, hashed password (never the password itself), language and theme.
  • Business records you enter: drinks and prices, stock movements, sales and their items, receipt numbers, shifts and cash counts, customer accounts and credit ledger, supplier and expense notes.
  • Device and technical data: app version, platform, an anonymous session identifier, error traces, and the amount/status of payment attempts.
  • Support messages you send us and the contact details in them.

We do not collect your address book, your SMS, or your location history, and there are no third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the Service — recording sales, syncing a Shop across its devices, printing receipts, reports and forecasts.
  • To keep accounts secure — sessions, roles, manager approvals, audit trails.
  • To manage subscriptions — which plan is active, when it expires, and the transaction that activated it.
  • To support you — diagnosing a problem you report.
  • To comply with the law — answering lawful requests from competent authorities.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with the providers needed to run the Service, each bound to use it for that purpose alone:

  • Hosting and database — our server host and managed Postgres provider, in an isolated, access-restricted project.
  • Payment providers — the mobile-money and card operators used for payments; they receive the amount, the reference and the payer’s phone or email, and have their own privacy policies.
  • Messaging — if a user taps “send on WhatsApp”, the message leaves the device to WhatsApp; we do not see the recipient.
  • Authorities — only where the law requires, and we will inform the Shop unless legally barred from doing so.

6. Where data is stored

Business data lives on servers and database infrastructure we manage, with access limited to the Shop’s own users and our technical operators. Part of it is also cached on the device so the till keeps selling without network (section 10). Where a transfer leaves Cameroon it is made only to providers under contractual safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

  • Active Shop data: for as long as the subscription is active.
  • After closure: deleted or irreversibly anonymised within 90 days, except what accounting or legal rules require us to keep (typically payment records).
  • Security and audit logs: up to 12 months.
  • Device caches: removed on sign-out and whenever the Shop changes.

8. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes; older rows are upgraded on the next successful sign-in.
  • Sessions are server-side and revocable, and the device PIN plus lock screen add a second barrier on a shared till.
  • Traffic is encrypted in transit, and public client keys cannot read the business tables (row-level security).
  • Every request is scoped on the server to the signed-in user’s Shop; manager-only actions — voiding an order, approving a customer, changing the company logo — are re-checked server-side and logged with who, when and why.
  • Payment webhooks are verified by signature before any plan is activated.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Cameroon’s Law No. 2010/012 on cybersecurity, you may ask us to access a copy of your personal data, correct anything inaccurate, delete your account and data, export it, restrict or object to a processing activity, or withdraw consent where we relied on it.

A Shop’s own customer who wants their tab data corrected or removed should ask that Shop first, since the Shop controls it; if the Shop cannot help, write to us and we will assist.

10. Device permissions

  • Camera — only when scanning a barcode/QR label or attaching a drink photo.
  • Photos/files — only to attach a drink picture or a company logo.
  • Bluetooth — only to print a receipt on a paired thermal printer.
  • Notifications — stock, expiry and shift reminders; switch them off in the app or Android settings.
  • Local storage — the offline cache that lets the till keep selling. It is scoped per Shop and cleared on sign-out.

You may refuse any permission; the matching feature simply stays unavailable.

11. Children

The Service is a professional business tool, not directed at children. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18, and staff accounts must be created by the Shop’s manager.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the Service evolves. The version and date at the top always reflect the current text, and a material change is announced in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
